## Step 1 — Preview Locally

1. Open the `jyoti-portfolio` folder on your computer
2. Double-click `index.html`
3. It opens directly in your browser — fully working

---

## Step 2 — Deploy on Netlify (Free, 3 minutes)

### Option A — Drag and Drop (Easiest, no account needed)

1. Go to → **jyotik.netlify.app **
2. Drag the entire `jyoti-portfolio` **folder** onto the page
3. Wait 10 seconds → your live URL appears
   Example: `https://luminous-fox-a1b2.netlify.app`
4. Share this URL anywhere

### Option B — Custom URL (Recommended)

1. Go to → **https://app.netlify.com** → Sign up free with Google/Email
2. Click **"Add new site"** → **"Deploy manually"**
3. Drag the `jyoti-portfolio` folder into the upload area
4. Once deployed, go to **Site configuration → Change site name**
5. Type: `jyoti-kumari` → Save
6. Your URL becomes → **https://jyotik.netlify.app/ ** ✓

## Step 3 — Add to LinkedIn (10 minutes)

After getting your live URL, do the following on LinkedIn:

### A) Featured Section (Most Visible to Recruiters)
1. Open your LinkedIn profile
2. Scroll to the **Featured** section
3. Click the **+** button → select **"Add a link"**
4. Paste your Netlify URL (e.g. https://jyotik.netlify.app/)
5. LinkedIn auto-generates a preview card → click **Save**

### B) Contact Info → Website
1. Click the **pencil icon** near your profile photo
2. Scroll down to **Website** → click **"Add website"**
3. Paste your URL → set Website type label to: **Portfolio**
4. Click **Save**

## Portfolio Features

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| Custom cursor | Dot + lagging ring, expands on hover |
| Typing animation | Cycles through 5 role titles in hero |
| Photo modal | Click photo → full intro popup opens |
| Floating badges | 3 animated stat cards around photo |
| Spinning gradient ring | Animated border around profile photo |
| Magnetic buttons | Buttons subtly follow cursor |
| Parallax tilt | Photo card tilts with mouse movement |
